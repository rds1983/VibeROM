# Races

ROM 2.4b6 has four playable races. Stats are fixed at creation: there is no
stat rolling or rerolling. Your class's prime attribute gains +3 when you
enter the world.

| Race | Points | STR | DEX | INT | WIS | CON | Size | Bonus skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | 0 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 | Medium | — |
| Elf | 5 | 12 | 15 | 14 | 13 | 11 | Small | sneak, hide |
| Dwarf | 8 | 14 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 15 | Medium | berserk |
| Giant | 6 | 16 | 11 | 11 | 13 | 14 | Large | bash, fast healing |

## Racial traits

**Human** — no special resistances or vulnerabilities; the cheapest race to
advance.

**Elf** — infravision, resists charm, vulnerable to iron, and starts with
sneak and hide. Elves are quick and intelligent but frail.

**Dwarf** — infravision, resists poison and disease, vulnerable to drowning,
and starts with berserk. Dwarves are sturdy and strong.

**Giant** — resists fire and cold, vulnerable to mental and lightning attacks,
and starts with bash and fast healing. Giants are the strongest race but pay
for it with slower advancement in most classes.

## Creation points and experience

Each race has a point cost (shown above) that raises the experience needed per
level. Class also modifies experience: for example, a giant mage needs twice
the base experience, while a human mage needs the base amount. The exact
multipliers are visible through the game's score/experience messages as those
commands are ported.

## Race help

At the race prompt, typing `help` repeats the list of available races. Full
race help entries arrive with the help system.
