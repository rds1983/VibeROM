# Classes

ROM 2.4b6 has four classes. Your class decides your prime attribute (+3 at
creation), your basic weapon, your starting skills and your title.

| Class | Prime attribute | Basic weapon | Title at level 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mage | Intelligence | dagger | Apprentice of Magic |
| Cleric | Wisdom | mace | Believer |
| Thief | Dexterity | dagger | Pilferer (Pilferess for female characters) |
| Warrior | Strength | sword | Swordpupil |

Every character also starts with `rom basics` (scrolls, staves, wands and
recall), recall at 50, 5 practices and 3 training sessions.

## Mage

Mages rely on spells. The basic package teaches dagger; the default package
adds lore and the beguiling, combat, detection, enhancement, illusion,
maladiction, protective, transportation and weather spell groups. Mages can
only choose a dagger as their starting weapon.

## Cleric

Clerics combine maces with healing and protective magic. The basic package
teaches mace; the default package adds flail and the attack, creation,
curative, benediction, detection, healing, maladiction, protective, shield
block, transportation and weather groups. Clerics may start with a mace or a
flail.

## Thief

Thieves are quick and sneaky. The basic package teaches dagger and steal; the
default package adds mace, sword, backstab, disarm, dodge, second attack,
trip, hide, peek, pick lock and sneak. Thieves may start with a sword, mace or
dagger.

## Warrior

Warriors have the most hit points and the widest weapon training. The basic
package teaches sword and second attack; the default package adds the
weaponsmaster group (axe, dagger, flail, mace, polearm, spear/staff, sword and
whip), shield block, bash, disarm, enhanced damage, parry, rescue and third
attack. Warriors may start with any of the eight weapon types.

## Advancement

Experience per level depends on race and class. Humans have the baseline
multiplier; elves, dwarves and giants pay more in some or all classes (see
[Races](Races.md)). A giant mage, for example, needs twice the base experience
of a human mage.
